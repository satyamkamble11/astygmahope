# Admin & Staff User Manual
## Astygma Hope Clinic - Receptionist & Admin Operations Guide

### 1. Reception Triage Workflow (No Payment Required)
1. **New Request Alert**: When a patient submits an appointment form on the website, a real-time entry appears in the Reception Portal queue (`/portal/reception`).
2. **Instant WhatsApp Direct Action**: Receptionists can click the **"Send WhatsApp Confirmation"** button next to any booking to automatically open WhatsApp Web / App with a pre-formatted message:
   > *"Hello [Patient Name], Astygma Hope Clinic Shirol has received your appointment request for [Service] on [Date] at [Time]. Dr. Umesh D. Kalekar's office confirms your visit. Phone: +91 7522900512."*
3. **Approval Status Toggle**: Click **Approve** or **Reschedule** to update patient status.

---

### 2. CMS Publishing Guide (Instagram Style)
1. Navigate to `/portal/admin` -> **CMS Management**.
2. Select Post Type: `Story`, `Carousel`, `Blog Post`, `Health Tip`, or `Festival Banner`.
3. Upload media (stored in `/assets/...`) and publish instantly.

---

### 3. Theme Manager & Festival Customization
1. Go to Admin Settings -> **Theme Manager**.
2. Toggle between **Light Luxury**, **Dark Obsidian**, **Diwali Gold**, or **Navratri Festival**.
3. Changes immediately apply site-wide for all visitors.
